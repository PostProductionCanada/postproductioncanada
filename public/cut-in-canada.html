🎞️ Did you know that was cut in Canada?

Movies and television whose editing, visual effects, sound or colour was done in Canada, often for directors and studios you wouldn't expect.

🇨🇦 📍 ON VFX Shot in Canada

Frankenstein (Guillermo del Toro, Netflix)

Toronto studio Mr. X delivered 950 effects shots over almost two years, November 2023 to September 2025, on del Toro's film. The opening ice scenes were filmed in a parking lot at Netflix's Toronto studio and finished with effects. Other studios, including ILM, also worked on the film.

  • Inside the VFX of Frankenstein with Mr. X Autodesk
  • Tamara Deverell on building Frankenstein Motion Picture Association
  • Behind the VFX of Frankenstein ILM
🇨🇦 📍 ON VFX

The Shape of Water, Crimson Peak, Nightmare Alley

Mr. X, the Toronto-based studio, did the effects on these earlier del Toro films too. Production visual effects supervisor Dennis Berardi is described as a longtime collaborator of del Toro's, and the studio relaunched as an independent brand in 2025.

  • MR. X returns: storied VFX studio relaunches as independent, artist-led brand Yahoo Finance
🇨🇦 📍 QC Editing

Heated Rivalry

Québec Editor Véronique Barbe IMDb cut the series and won Best Editing in a TV Drama or Mini Series at the 2026 CCE Awards, plus a Canadian Screen Awards sweep for the show.

🇨🇦 📍 BC VFX

Tron: Ares, The Odyssey, The Fantastic Four: First Steps

A Vancouver VFX studio guide lists these among recent studio features with effects work from Vancouver studios: ILM and Image Engine on Tron: Ares, DNEG on The Odyssey, and Digital Domain on The Fantastic Four: First Steps. It is a directory rather than a credits database, so confirm credits before you quote them.
