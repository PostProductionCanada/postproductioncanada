Canadian Cinema Editors (CCE)

Logo © Canadian Cinema Editors
Non-profit professional association

The CCE is a bilingual, not-for-profit professional, educational and cultural association for Canada-wide post-production people, founded in 2007 and based in Toronto. It exists to promote picture editing in film, television and new media, to educate peers and students, and to show Canadian editors' work to the world. It is not a union. It runs the CCE Awards, the annual EditCon conference, mentorship, a podcast and an editor directory.

West coast: Vancouver Post Alliance

Non-profit association, British Columbia

The Vancouver Post Alliance (VPA) is a non-profit association that represents BC's post-production industry. It began as an ad hoc steering committee in spring 2013 and was established as a non-profit society a few months later. It promotes and markets BC's post talent, runs professional development and networking, and speaks for the industry to government. Its lobbying helped get post-production services added to BC's DAVE tax credit on March 1, 2015.
