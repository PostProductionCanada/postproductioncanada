🎧 Sound Editing and Design

The people who make Canadian film and television sound right.

Sound editing and sound design is the part of post where dialogue is cleaned and replaced, effects and Foley are built and cut, and the world of the film or series is made audible, before a re-recording mixer balances it all in the final mix. In Canada the work is celebrated in its own awards: the Canadian Screen Awards have separate categories for Best Sound Editing and Best Sound Mixing.

Facts on this page were read on November 20, 2026 from the pages linked. Canadian only. If something is out of date, or you know a Canadian sound Editor, designer or mixer with a story worth telling, send it to us.

Canadian sound people

Jane Tattersall

Supervising sound Editor and sound designer, Toronto

Born in England, she moved to Canada as a child and studied at Queen's University. She worked as a researcher on TVOntario documentaries before apprenticing as a sound Editor, then formed her own company, Tattersall Sound. Wikipedia describes her as a six-time Genie Award and Canadian Screen Award winner for Best Sound Editing. Her first Genie win was for Naked Lunch, where she reportedly had to put her hand in a jar of cockroaches to get the hissing sound she wanted. In January 2023, Streamland Media named her managing director of Picture Shop and senior vice president of Formosa Group in Toronto.

Christian Cooke and Brad Zoern

Re-recording mixers, Toronto: Oscar-nominated twice

Cooke and Zoern were nominated for the Academy Award for Best Sound Mixing for The Shape of Water at the 90th Academy Awards, along with production mixer Glen Gauthier. KawarthaNOW, Cooke's hometown outlet in Lindsay, Ontario, reported that Cooke and Zoern work as re-recording mixers at Deluxe Toronto. They were nominated again for Frankenstein in Best Sound at the 98th Academy Awards. F1 won that category. Zoern was born in Windsor, Ontario.

Glen Gauthier

Production sound mixer, Ontario

A Canadian location sound mixer who has worked since 1978 and was nominated alongside Cooke and Zoern for The Shape of Water. His Wikipedia entry lists Academy Award, BAFTA, Gemini and Genie nominations.
