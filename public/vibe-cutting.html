🎚️ Vibe Cutting

Vibe cutting is editing by describing what you want in plain language and letting an AI carry out the steps in the software. The name borrows from "vibe coding," the idea of building software by describing it instead of writing the code. In editing, the Editor still decides what the cut should feel like. The AI takes on the mechanical work: organizing bins, making transcripts, building a first assembly, cleaning up.

Items marked vendor come from companies that sell a related product, so read them with that in mind. Summaries are mine, and each item links to its source.

What people mean by vibe editing

  • What is vibe editing? Tellers.AI glossary vendor
    A short definition: expressing editorial intent in natural language while an AI video agent does the production work.
  • Vibe Editing: the complete guide to AI-driven video editing Pexo vendor
    Describes re-editing existing footage by describing the mood and style you want, and traces the term back to vibe coding.
  • You can use AI to edit videos for you, here's how Forward Future
    A professional Editor's guide to handing AI the busywork, directing it in plain English and keeping your taste in charge.
  • Vibe editing turns the video Editor into a conversation TechBullion vendor
    A startup that edits by chat shows its plan before applying it and lets you undo each step. Treat it as company coverage.

The software is opening up to AI

  • Premiere AI Assistant (beta) overview Adobe Help, 2026-06-18
    Adobe's own description: with plain-language prompts the assistant organizes bins, makes transcripts, detects slates, adds markers and builds stringouts and rough assemblies inside Premiere.
  • Adobe's Premiere updates bring more AI into the editing timeline DPReview
    The assistant gets more capable, Editors can save repeatable instructions as slash-command "skills," and Adobe previews local MCP connections so third-party AI agents can work in native Premiere projects.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro gets an AI agent, generative media and a new Color Mode Creative Bloq
    A hands-on look at the agent applying effects across a whole sequence, plus the question of who used to do that repetitive work.
  • A quick look at how Adobe's new AI Assistant could streamline your workflows in Premiere No Film School
    A practical take that notes the assistant uses Firefly generative credits, so it isn't free.
  • DaVinci Resolve 21.1 uses MCP to let AI assistants control the edit YMCinema, 2026-09-17
    Resolve Studio now has a built-in MCP server, so an AI assistant can drive Resolve through its API.
  • DaVinci Resolve 21.1: AI editing with Claude and ChatGPT, tested YouTube
    A hands-on test. It explains that the agent works through Resolve's API, not the on-screen interface, so it can only do what the API allows, and that this needs the Studio version.
  • What's new in Avid Media Composer 2026.8 Avid, 2026-08-31 vendor
    Multicam Auto Cut driven by who is speaking, plus better transcript tools. Avid calls the direction agentic editing.
  • Autodesk adds AI assistant, MCP servers and Arnold viewport updates Post Perspective, 2026-11-18
    Autodesk joins the move to AI assistants and MCP connections. See Post Perspective's latest for the full story.
  • IBC2026: A-Z of exhibitor news SVG Europe
    Shows how widespread the shift is: for example, SNS's on-premise AI suite takes plain-language requests and can assemble an EDL from matching clips.

AI beyond the edit: footage and discovery

  • Google Flow: 5 new AI tools for visual storytelling (Pocket Studio) Google, 2026-11-12
    Google's announcement. Pocket Studio generates several camera angles from one phone take and upgrades the image, and Google says it keeps the actor's original performance intact. That is Google's claim, not an independent test. It matters to Editors because coverage is what they cut with.
  • Google adds five artist-built Flow tools for characters, effects and camera angles Superpower Daily, 2026-11-12 vendor
    A summary that stresses the new views are generated from the recording rather than captured by extra cameras, and that the claims about preserving performance are not independently measured.
  • Netflix Launches AI Discover Content Recommendation Feature Variety, 2026-11-03
    Netflix's AI Discover tab, on the TV app for a limited group of US subscribers, offers real-time themed picks such as Scare Me and Family Movie Night from your viewing history. Variety says it is not a chatbot and is not on kids' profiles. Netflix calls it a test kitchen. It changes how audiences find what Editors cut.
  • Can't Find Anything to Watch on Netflix? This New AI Feature May Be the Answer PCMag UK, 2026-11 vendor
    Describes where the Discover tab appears in the TV app, the Tune Your Recommendations feedback control, and Netflix's own wording about a new intelligence layer that responds in real time.

What it means for Editors

  • AI in Post-Production: how to use it and where it saves time Runway vendor
    Argues AI saves the most time on ingest, assembly and cleanup, while picture lock and the final mix stay mostly human decisions.
  • Will AI replace video Editors? What the Post-Production data shows Coursiv, 2026-09-10 vendor
    Argues the mechanical layer of editing has largely been automated, and the market is splitting. It uses US employment figures.

The Canadian angle

  • Directors Guild of Canada calls for AI protections The Hollywood Reporter
    The DGC, which represents Editors among others, issued a manifesto at the Banff World Media Festival on consent, attribution and control over creative work in the age of AI.
