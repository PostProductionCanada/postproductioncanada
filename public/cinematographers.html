📷 Cinematographers

The Canadians who photograph the screen.

The cinematographer, also called the director of photography or DP, photographs the film and runs the camera and lighting teams. The Editor then shapes those images into the story. The two never work in the same room at the same time, but each depends on the other, and the best results come when they talk early.

Facts were read on November 20, 2026 from the linked pages. This is a short list, not the full list: send us a Canadian cinematographer to add.

Canadian cinematographers

📍 Ontario

Paul Sarossy

Born in Barrie, Ontario, and a York University graduate. He is credited CSC, BSC and ASC. He has shot fourteen features for director Atom Egoyan, among them Exotica, The Sweet Hereafter, Felicia's Journey and Ararat, and has won five Genie Awards for Best Achievement in Cinematography.

Official site · Wikipedia

📍 Québec

Guy Dufaux

Born in Lille, France, in 1943. He immigrated to Canada in 1965 and became a Canadian citizen in 1971. Most of his work has been in Canadian cinema. Recognition includes a Genie nomination for Stardom and the Canadian Society of Cinematographers' Kodak New Century Award in 2002.

Wikipedia

📍 Québec

Nicolas Bolduc

Born in Montréal in 1973. He won the Canadian Screen Award for Best Cinematography two years in a row, for War Witch (2012) and Enemy (2013), and won again for Hochelaga, Land of Souls at the 6th Canadian Screen Awards. He also won a Jutra Award for War Witch.

Wikipedia

📍 Québec

André Turpin

A Québec cinematographer, director and screenwriter, born in 1966. As a cinematographer he has won over a dozen awards, including Canadian Screen Awards for Best Cinematography for his work on Xavier Dolan IMDb's films Mommy and It's Only the End of the World.

Wikipedia

The society

Canadian Society of Cinematographers (CSC)

Toronto, founded 1957

A not-for-profit organization dedicated to fostering cinematography in Canada since 1957. Its site lists a member directory, workshops, mentorship, a library of talks and podcasts, and its yearly CSC Awards. Wikipedia reports more than 800 members, and members use the letters CSC after their names.

DPs and Editors

The image and the cut tell the story together. These guides are about getting the two crafts talking before the shoot, not after.

Effective pre-production collaboration between the cinematographer and Editor

PremiumBeat

Argues that the DP and Editor work in different phases of a project, so collaborating early gives a better final film. A short guide to what each role does and how to plan the shots together.

Dailies best practices

Netflix Backlot

Netflix's guide to dailies recommends workflow meetings that include cinematographers and Editors, because the colour plan decides whether the dailies operator applies CDLs or LUTs.
