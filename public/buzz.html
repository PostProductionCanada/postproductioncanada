🎬 Post Buzz

What's heading into post across Canada. When a production wraps, the edit, colour, sound and VFX come next, so end-of-shoot dates show where the work is about to land.

What might be coming, from the unions' own public listings. These are production titles, studios and dates only, as of November 19, 2026. Schedules move, and a listing is not a promise of post work, so confirm with the production.
