🏆 Awards honouring Editors

The awards that celebrate the people who shape the story in post, and the recent Canadian winners.

The Editor is an artist

Editing is not assembly. It is where a film’s story, rhythm and emotion are decided. A long-standing filmmaking adage calls editing the final rewrite.

⏱️

Rhythm

The pacing of the cuts sets the mood: quick cuts build urgency, longer ones let a moment breathe.

🎭

Performance

Choosing which take, and when to cut to a reaction, can change what a line or a look means.

✂️

What stays out

Some of the biggest decisions are omissions. What an Editor leaves out can hit harder than what is shown.

Paraphrased from The Film Student’s Guide to Visual Storytelling (L.A. Film School) and Your Performance in the Editing Room.

The awards that honour the craft

Canadian Cinema Editors Awards

CCE

Canada's Editors honour their own. The 16th edition in 2026 presented 17 awards, including a Lifetime Achievement Award and a Career Achievement Award.

Canadian Screen Awards

Academy of Canadian Cinema & Television

Best Editing has been presented since 1966, first under the Canadian Film Awards. There is a separate documentary editing category, plus sound editing and visual effects. Nominees in editing are chosen by the Academy's Cinema Editors branch. The rules say Post Production Supervisor is not an eligible credit for sound mixing or sound editing.

DGC Awards

Directors Guild of Canada

The guild's awards, including picture editing and sound editing categories for film and TV. The 24th edition was held November 8, 2025 at Koerner Hall in Toronto.

Canadian Sync Awards

Guild of Music Supervisors, Canada

Honours music supervision in Canadian film, TV and media. The 2026 awards took place June 11 in Toronto.

Eddie Awards

American Cinema Editors

The American Cinema Editors' awards for editing. International.

Academy Awards: Film Editing

Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

The Oscar for Best Film Editing. International.

VES Awards

Visual Effects Society

International awards for visual effects.

I found no awards show that honours post supervisors specifically. If you know of one, send it and I will check it.
