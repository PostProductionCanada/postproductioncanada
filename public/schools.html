🎓 Education across Canada

Where to learn picture editing, sound and Post-Production, from Newfoundland to Vancouver. Colleges, universities and private schools.

Programs change every year, so check each school before you apply. Being listed is not an endorsement. Where we couldn't reach a school's own page, the entry says so. Visual effects, colour and music programs have been added, but the list is not complete, and we don't know the full count of Canadian schools that teach these. New Brunswick, PEI and the territories aren't covered yet.

🛠️ Learn on the job: mentorships, trainee roles and internships

We found few public sound or colour internship programs run by Canadian facilities. Most of this work is learned as an assistant or trainee and comes through word of mouth. Here is what is documented.

🎞️ Picture🎧 Sound🧭 Post supervision
DGC trainee positions in post
The 2023–25 DGC Ontario schedule has a letter agreeing that a Tier A production doing post in Ontario will engage a trainee from one of three streams (picture editing, sound editing, or Post-Production supervision and coordination) for six weeks or the length of post, whichever is less. A matching letter is in the 2026–28 Maritimes agreement. Source
🎞️ Picture🧭 Post producing
CCE Mentorship Program
A one-year program. Mentee categories are assistant editing, VFX editing, scripted picture editing, documentary picture editing and post-producing. The FAQ says it is open to CCE Associate or Full Members who live in Ontario, with at least two years in film and television, and who are not in school. The CCE also runs speed-mentoring and small-group sessions for emerging and mid-career Editors and assistants. Source
🎞️ Picture🎧 Sound🟩 VFX
Vancouver Post Alliance mentorship
Includes Speed Mentoring, where mentees rotate through picture Editors, re-recording mixers, VFX producers, post producers and composers, a Spring Mentorship Program, and a year-round Coffee With a Mentor program that it says is open to members and non-members with no minimum experience. Source. Its post audio career page says supervisors, sound designers and re-recording mixers need the most experience, while sound assistants are entry level.
🎨 Colour🎧 Sound🧭 Finishing
Company 3 NExT internship
A paid, 10-week, full-time summer internship with tracks that include Post-Production finishing (colour and finishing), VFX and sound. Positions may be on site, remote or hybrid. Company 3 also lists a NExT Apprenticeship Program. We could not confirm that Toronto positions are offered, so ask the company. Source
🎨 Colour
How one Canadian colourist started
Company 3's biography of Toronto senior colourist Dave Muscat IMDb says he joined what was then Deluxe Toronto in 2000 as a colour assist, learned foundational techniques from senior artists, then worked as a finishing Editor on many shows before becoming a colourist. It is one documented path, not the only one. Source
🎞️ Picture🎧 Sound
An Academy apprenticeship, 1988
The CCE says Annie Jean, its 2026 Lifetime Achievement recipient, began as a scholarship recipient of the Academy of Canadian Cinema & Television's National Apprenticeship Program in 1988. We could not confirm whether that program is still running. Source

🎬 James Cameron: Canadian, but not a Canadian film-school story

Born in Kapuskasing, Ontario, Cameron grew up near Niagara Falls and attended Stamford Collegiate there. His family moved to California when he was 17. He enrolled at Fullerton College in 1973 to study physics, switched to English and left in 1974. He then taught himself special effects, partly by reading graduate theses in a university library. So he was schooled in Ontario, but not in film.

Source: Wikipedia · Academy of Achievement
